    Updating Accounts for Wellness Center Access

    Issue/Question

    I can't get into the Wellness Center when I swipe

    Requesting that new accounts be imported

    Load Fusion

    Can you please make sure all Faculty/Staff accounts are updated in Fusion for this month?

    Error: Unable to find user matching the ID:

    Environment

    • Fusion
    • Wellness Center Software
    • wellness.usd.edu

    Cause

    Account information is missing in Fusion

    Student has not registered for classes

    Resolution

    1. Access to the Wellness Center for students is based off of registration for classes
      • If you are not registered, please contact your advisor to get registered for classes 
    2. Please contact the Service Desk with the following information: 
      1. Issue you are experiencing
      2. When you registered for classes (if student) 
      3. Timeline for update

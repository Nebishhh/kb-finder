    Receiving Page Not Found Error when Playing Video in D2L

    Issue/Question

    I am unable to access the video linked in D2L. 

    Error: Page Not Found - The page you are looking for cannot be found

    Environment

    • Streaming Media server
    • Desire2Learn (D2L) 

    Cause

    The link in Desire2Learn is pointing to a previous course's materials.  Students will not be able to view the video until the link is corrected. 

    Resolution

    Note: These steps need to be performed by the instructor

    1. Log in to D2L
    2. Go to the Video under the Content Section
    3. If there are two options to Edit Link, Click Edit Link for the one beginning with https://d2l.sdbor.edu
    4. You will need to copy the correct link and update the one beginning with https://d2l.sdbor.edu

    Note: The correct link should not begin with https://d2l.sdbor.edu 

    Please contact the Service Desk if you need further assistance

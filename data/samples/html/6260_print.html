Coyote Card Mobile Not Working

Summary

What to do if your Coyote Card Mobile is not working. error Your login is correct, but your account has not been properly registered in your school's Transact system and we cannot access your account information.

Body

Issue/Question

I loaded Coyote Card mobile app but it isn't working

There is no option to Add the card to Apple Wallet in the eAccounts app

I am unable to use my Coyote Card Mobile

Error: Sorry something went wrong:  Your login is correct, but your account has not been properly registered in your school's Transact system and we cannot access your account information.  Please contact your campus program administrator or card office to resolve this issue.

Environment

  • Coyote Card Mobile
  • Transact

Resolution

  1. If you have received a new phone follow Removing Coyote Card Mobile ID to remove the card from your old device
  2. Check to see if your card has been deactivated in the eAccounts Application OR eAccounts Web Version
    1. eAccounts Application
      1. Click settings gear in the upper right corner
      2. Select Card Management option
      3. Choose credential to deactivate 
      4. Toggle to Off
        Note:  You will receive a confirmation email,  Only the selected card will be deactivated,  All other credentials will remain active
    2. eAccounts web version
      1. Select Card Services
      2. Select Deactivate Card
      3. Select credential to deactivate
      4. Click on Deactivate Card
        Note:  You will receive a confirmation email, Only the selected card will be deactivated,  All other credentials will remain active
  3. Please contact the Coyote Card office with the following information: 
    1. Name
    2. Student ID

ITS STAFF ONLY

  1. Follow Enabling Coyote Card Mobile
  2. Follow Troubleshooting eAccounts Mobile Error Something Went Wrong
